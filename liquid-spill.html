Liquid Spill Recovery

Spilled liquid on your laptop? Turn it off immediately and bring it to us.

Call Labeeb

What's Involved

Liquid spills require immediate attention. We disassemble the laptop, extract the logic board, clean corrosion in an ultrasonic bath using chemical solvents, and repair burned track pins under a microscope to restore functionality and recover your data.

Signs You Need This Service

  • Coffee, tea, water, or wine spilled onto laptop keyboard
  • Device shutting down immediately post spill or failing to start
  • Sticky keys or unresponsive trackpad behaviors after moisture
  • Corrosion visual inside port openings or SIM slot areas
